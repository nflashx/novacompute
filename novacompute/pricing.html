Pricing FAQ

How is billing calculated exactly?

Input and output tokens are metered separately and summed at the rates above. Prompt caching and failed requests are not billed. One balance covers every model.

Do you charge for embeddings or image models?

Chat and code models are live today; embeddings and vision pricing will be published on this page when those models ship. Existing keys work with new models automatically.

Why are your rates lower than the providers'?

We buy capacity in bulk and route traffic efficiently across providers. As a reseller we pass most of that margin on to you — the numbers on this page are our actual rates.

What happens when my balance hits $0?

Paid models return a 402 until you top up; free-tier models like Qwen 3.7 keep working. We email you before the balance runs out if usage alerts are enabled.
