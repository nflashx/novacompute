Pricing FAQ

How is billing calculated exactly?

Input and output tokens are metered separately and summed at the rates above. Prompt caching and failed requests are not billed. One balance covers every model.

Do you charge for embeddings or image models?

We don't sell embeddings. Image input is included in the normal per-token price for models marked IMG; video-capable models are marked VID. There is no separate vision surcharge.

Why are your rates lower than the providers'?

We benchmark against OpenRouter and OpenCode's listed rates: Claude Opus/Sonnet, GPT-6, DeepSeek 1017 and GLM Flash are roughly 10–25% below them. A few models (Grok, Kimi, Gemini Argon) are even lower because we route through limited-time partner capacity. The numbers on this page are our actual sell rates — no hidden markup at checkout.

What happens when my balance hits $0?

Paid models return a 402 until you top up; free-tier models like Qwen 3.7 keep working. We email you before the balance runs out if usage alerts are enabled.
